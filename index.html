01 — JPMorgan Chase

Mapping the IPO journey end-to-end

ECM bankers navigate one of the most complex, high-stakes deal processes in finance — and most of that complexity lived only in their heads. We ran two intensive working sessions with senior ECM executives to surface, document, and map the full lifecycle of an IPO deal. The research reached the #3 product executive at the firm, reporting directly to Jamie Dimon, and drove six downstream initiatives across platform strategy, AI, and onboarding.

02 — Autograph

From collectibles to fan experiences

Autograph's NFT platform was losing users and community sentiment was tanking. A three-phase zero-to-one research program — 11 interviews, 4 focus groups, and an n=4,000 survey — revealed fans didn't want more digital collectibles. They wanted to get closer to their favorite athletes in real life. The research pivoted the company's entire product strategy.

02 — About

Research as a strategic discipline

I'm a UX Research Lead based in Los Angeles with 8+ years working at the intersection of complex systems and human behavior. I've led research at JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Autograph, and SalesLoft — ranging from zero-to-one platform exploration to large-scale mixed-methods programs that shaped product roadmaps and executive strategy.

My work spans enterprise workflows, consumer financial products, and fan engagement platforms. Regardless of domain, my focus is the same: build understanding fast, synthesize with rigor, and communicate in a way that makes it impossible not to act.

Outside of work, you'll find me on the golf course, out for a run, or deep in a manga or anime series.
